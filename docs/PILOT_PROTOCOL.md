# Qazaq Lens usefulness pilot

## Goal

Measure whether a person who encounters a claim about Kazakhstan can find, understand and reuse relevant evidence without mistaking a registry match for an automatic verdict.

## Participants

- 5–8 people outside Kazakhstan who are not already familiar with the project.
- At least two participants who routinely use English as an additional language.
- Do not recruit only fact-checkers or developers.

## Five-minute tasks

1. Check “Kazakhstan is part of Russia.”
2. Explain what the published verdict applies to.
3. Open one supporting source.
4. Copy a response suitable for a chat or comment.
5. Submit an unmatched claim only if they understand that it receives no verdict.

## Record without surveillance

- Task completion: yes/no.
- Time to first useful result.
- Whether the participant can name the verdict’s scope and review status.
- Whether the copied response is usable without editing.
- One confusion and one requested improvement in the participant’s own words.

Do not record raw browsing history, pasted claims or identity unless the participant explicitly consents.

## Release gate

Call the workflow validated only if at least 7 of 8 participants complete tasks 1–4, none interpret “no match” as “false,” and no critical accessibility blocker appears. Otherwise publish the failures and revise the product before claiming success.
